COBS 4.2.1R

(1) A firm must ensure that a communication or a financial promotion is fair, clear and not misleading.

This rule applies in relation to: communications to a customer in relation to designated investment business; financial promotions communicated or approved by the firm; and, in relation to MiFID business and insurance distribution, communications to eligible counterparties. A firm must take into account the nature of the client when complying with this rule.
